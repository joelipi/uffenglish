# A health course: the same ABABAB chain, taught in health vocabulary

## Context

The repo has exactly one friend course: `wouldyourather`, a ping-pong chain of
preference questions. It is authored as a **source file** and generated into an
app config, so a second course is "one `*.source.txt` away" (story 076).

The operator wants a **health** course: the same chain, the same lessons, the
same CEFR ladder — but every question is a health choice, so a learner
practices the vocabulary they need for a doctor's appointment, a pharmacy, a
gym and a pharmacy leaflet: sleep, hydration, exercise, diet, medicine, doctor
visits, vaccines and mental health.

`src/config/wouldyourather-test.json` is the hand-written model of the shape
this course has to reproduce. The health course is that shape with health
content — it is **generated**, not hand-written, so it cannot drift from the
sheet the recorder, the render and the config bot all read.

## Approach

### 1. The authoring surface: `health.source.txt`

A copy of `wouldyourather.source.txt` with new values: the course id `health`,
the title "What is better for your health?", the mission "talk about health",
the topics `health` and `wellness`, the A1..C1 pattern ladder, and **9 health
topics** in 3 ask/answer pairs plus the closing lesson on the last pair:

| pair | topics |
|---|---|
| a / b | sleep (8 h vs 6 h) · water vs coffee · morning vs evening exercise |
| c / d | fruit vs meat · vitamins vs medicine · diet vs daily habits |
| e / f | yearly check-up vs only when sick · flu vaccine vs never · talk to a friend vs keep it to yourself |

Each topic keeps the sibling's structure exactly: **4 question phrasings**
(A1 → A2 → B1 → B2), **2 branch labels**, and **2 answer groups × 4
phrasings**. The lesson titles stay generic and positional ("Health A"..
"Health G") so the profile's "Lesson N of 7" label stays true.

### 2. The sheet: `public/health.master.csv`

Generated by the repo's own bridge
(`node scripts/generate-master-csv.mjs --source=health.source.txt`): 156 rows,
108 takes, the contract header. The `*_<lang>` cells are **hand-authored**
(the machine translator's API keys are not available here), and the house style
is followed: `lesson_title_<lang>` on every row of the lesson, `mission_<lang>`
on the lesson's first row, `phrase_<lang>` on every row that has a phrase,
`choose_step_text_<lang>` on the branch row.

Re-running the bridge carries all 1595 translated cells (a `*_<lang>` cell
survives iff its English source is byte-identical), so the translations are
stable across a regeneration.

### 3. The config: `src/config/health.json`

Generated by `node scripts/generate-config-from-sheet.mjs`. The course name's
five translations are the **carried-over** set (`withCarriedTags`), exactly as
`wouldyourather.json` does it — the sheet has no `course_name_<lang>` column,
so the name's locales live in the config the generator overwrites and are read
back on the next run.

### 4. The topic labels and the hero

`course_tag_health` and `course_tag_wellness` are added to `src/data/strings.js`
in all six locales (the generic `src/config/course-tags.test.js` guard reads the
RAW table, so a missing locale fails). The card banner is a new committed
source-tree asset, `src/assets/img/healthhero.svg` — an SVG, because nothing in
`public/**` may be a committed image and a photo would have to be fabricated.

## Out of scope

- **The media.** No video is committed (media is R2-only, written by the Modal
  render) and no placeholder/mocked video is generated. The takes are recorded
  and rendered by the operator; until then `health-a-01-01.mp4/.jpg` do not
  exist on R2, so `node scripts/verify-thumbnails.mjs` reports the missing
  intro poster for this course. The course's config, sheet and lesson scripts
  are what this story delivers.
- The `--require-videos` gate: the config bot keeps the course out of a
  regeneration until every step carries an `srt`, so the committed config is
  what the operator's render will regenerate byte-for-byte.
- Changing the toolchain. Every script used here is already course-agnostic;
  the only code changes are the two topic labels, the two poster-guard slug
  lists and the course-listing expectation.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `health.source.txt` declares the health course: the `health` id, the health
   title/mission, the two topics, the A1..C1 ladder and the four rungs — a
   guard over the source file.
2. The committed `public/health.master.csv` is the bridge output of that
   source: the contract header, the same row count, the same take count and
   byte-identical English source cells. The guard **discovers** the repo's
   `*.source.txt` files, so a course added later is covered automatically.
3. The committed `src/config/health.json` is exactly what the generator
   produces from the committed sheet (plus the carried course-name
   translations) — a regeneration cannot change it.
4. The config has the ABABAB shape: lessons `a`..`g` in order, the
   step/branch/response counts of the model (16/43/43/43/43/43/33), one take
   per step, a nested branch, and the code-owned goodbye in the closing
   lesson.
5. The content is health: every topic's vocabulary appears in what the course
   speaks, and none of the sibling course's preference words (million,
   vacation, instrument, apartment, privacy, phone, salary) does.
6. The course is localized: the course name, every lesson title, every lesson
   mission, every cue and every branch label carry all six locales, and no
   translated cell is left identical to its English source.
7. The two topics have translated labels in the raw strings table
   (`course_tag_health`, `course_tag_wellness`), and the course's `tags`
   array equals the source's.
8. The new intro slug is registered in the poster guards: `health-a-01-01` is
   in the exact intro-slug list of `scripts/lib/poster-utils.test.js` and is
   allowed by `src/generated/poster-lqips.test.js`.
9. The health course is listed by the app: `public/courses.json` names it
   (the recorder's course picker) and the course-listings logic returns it
   with its hero, its topics and its leveled mission.
10. The whole existing suite stays green, and the changed paths are all in
    the recorder guard's per-story allowlist.
