# UFF — Ultra Fast Fluency

**UFF (Ultra Fast Fluency)** is a web app for learning English fluency through speaking and listening. Learners work through video lessons built around a native-speaker role play, answer voice-first, and receive AI evaluation of intent/grammar plus a fluency score (0–100). It is built for learners who want to speak rather than tap through drills. Learners record themselves per step and receive a shareable end-of-lesson recap video; the friend-challenge loop lets a learner invite a friend to answer their recorded questions within a 48-hour window.

## Features

- **Voice-first role-play lessons** — native-speaker video sets the context, the learner answers by voice, and speech-to-text plus AI feedback scores each response ([answer-flow](tests/answer-flow.spec.js)).
- **Bilingual overlays** — cues and subtitles localized to the learner's L1 (en/es/pt/fr, plus targeted hi/bn keys).
- **Auto-generated simple-video captions** — new `simpleVideoUrl` videos get English SRT from local Whisper plus Gemini translations to es/pt/fr/hi/bn, committed to the branch on push ([story](stories/009-auto-caption-simple-videos/story.md)).
- **Lesson recap video** — client-side stitched end-of-lesson recap (model prompts + own recordings) with a fluency score card, generated via canvas + MediaRecorder.
- **Recap header clears the viewing browser's chrome** — the localized header banner sits below a top margin equal to 8% of the frame height, filled with the banner's own gradient, so the address bar / status bar of the browser the recap is played in no longer covers it ([story](stories/057-recap-header-top-margin/story.md)).
- **Stale-deploy recovery** — if a new deployment replaces the hashed JS chunks while a tab is still open, the next failed dynamic import triggers a bounded one-time reload (max 2 per tab) so the learner gets the new build instead of a `'text/html' is not a valid JavaScript MIME type` error ([story](stories/030-reload-stale-chunk/story.md)).
- **Recap subtitles always burn the matched cue for closed responses** — a learner's recorded answer for a closed-response step is subtitled with the matched cue variant when their transcript matched one (the canonical answer from `src/config`) and with no subtitle when none matched, but never the raw speech-to-text transcript, so recognition errors do not appear on the exported video; the per-segment R2 clips use the same plan ([story](stories/028-burn-cue-not-transcription/story.md)).
- **Friend clips keep their own caption in the recap** — a `remote` segment whose clip is a friend's published UGC video (slug ending `-response-NN`, i.e. a friend lesson's recorded prompts) gets no subtitle from the recap, because that clip already carries its own speaker's caption burned in when it was published per-segment; overlaying the response step's cue would stamp the current user's answer onto the friend's question video. The current user's own `webcam` segments keep the matched-cue rule ([story](stories/036-fix-friend-lesson-subtitles/story.md)).
- **Friend-recorded clips never show app subtitles** — a step whose clip is a friend's published UGC video (slug ending `-response-NN`) already carries its speaker's caption burned into the video, so the config generator emits no `subtitles` for it and the player never draws a second caption over the friend's clip; this fixes the `wouldyourather` lesson `b` `branching` step, whose master-sheet `srt` cell had produced a duplicate SRT over the friend's question clip. The `sync-srt` write-back also keys cells by step (`video_file`, else the `filename` prefix) instead of `filename`, so a rendered group's SRT is no longer stamped onto unrelated `filename`-less rows ([story](stories/057-branching-friend-videos-subtitles/story.md)).
- **Self-documenting overlay master** — the seeded master carries a `row_purpose` informational column describing the config output each row produces (`→ simpleVideoUrl`, `→ cue`, `→ chooseStep`, `→ subtitles`, …), with a friend UGC step flagged as `no app subtitles (caption burned in)`; it is ignored by the generator and translator ([story](stories/057-branching-friend-videos-subtitles/story.md)).
- **Concatenated recap published to R2** — when a logged-in learner finishes a lesson that publishes segments, the stitched recap is also uploaded to R2 under the same `videos/` namespace (48h TTL) as `videos/<shareCode>-<courseId>-<lessonId>-complete.mp4`, for every lesson including the friend ask/answer lessons `a`/`b`; the upload is best-effort and skipped when the transcoded file exceeds the Function's 50 MB cap ([story](stories/021-upload-concatenated-videos-to-r2/story.md)).
- **Per-segment clips published by trimming the recap recording** — when a logged-in learner finishes a lesson that publishes clips, each publishable webcam step's time range is recorded during the single recap render, and the per-segment clips are produced by trimming that recording (`WebCodecs`/`mediabunny`) and uploading them to R2; no second render pass is run. The clips carry the recap overlay (the share headline on `w`/`wf`, and the opening fluency card on the first clip) and keep the 1080×1920 composite with burned (dynamic) cues ([story](stories/035-single-pass-clip-publish/story.md)).
- **Prominent concat button on the success screen** — the end-of-lesson "make my video" trigger stays hidden while the short success clip plays, then is revealed as a large, glowing CONTINUE call-button under a water overlay that invites the learner to create and share their video, matching the earlier decision steps ([story](stories/011-enlarge-concat-button/story.md)).
- **Audible auto-playing after-video loop** — once the concatenated recap is ready, a display-only tail video (`aftersuccess`, swapped to `aftershare` on the Share tap) loops audibly with no second tap by continuing the gesture-unlocked render session (recorder already stopped, so the tail never enters the shared blob); both resolve per user language (`aftersuccess-es`, `aftershare-bn`, …) with the header banner's guest-first + English fallback, tap pauses/resumes, background play is kept where the OS permits, and any failure falls back to the blob preview. The success-screen Share button delivers via the native sheet on iOS/Android and via direct mp4 download (download icon) on Windows/Mac/Chromebook/Linux, where desktop share sheets are unreliable. Delivered and published files share one identity: `<shareCode>[-<askerCode>]-<courseId>-<lessonId>-complete.mp4`, with both contributors named on friend-sourced lessons ([story](stories/060-autoplay-share-video/story.md)).
- **Water glisten confined to the water** — the reusable `.water-surface` sheen (blue gradient + animated white `waterShimmer`) is layered behind its content, so it only shows where the blue water colour is actually visible; the opaque shells of non-lesson pages (`/`, `/home`, `/profile`, `/:shareCode`) cover the frame's blue, so they no longer shimmer across the whole page ([story](stories/050-water-glisten-blue-only/story.md)).
- **Landscape recording warning** — on iOS/Android, a dismissible banner tells the learner to rotate the device to portrait while it is held landscape, so recorded clips keep the intended 9:16 framing ([story](stories/015-warn-landscape-recording/story.md)).
- **A camera freeze can no longer ship a silent one-frame clip** — the 9:16 capture composite on Android now swallows a `drawImage` failure so a transiently data-less source can no longer kill the repaint loop and freeze the take on its first frame, appends its canvas to the DOM like every other capture canvas in the codebase, and stops destroying itself when the speech engine is still loading (that abort fires only on a lesson's first response and only on Android, and with nothing encoded yet it needlessly forced a mid-flow rebuild and a second `getUserMedia` milliseconds before recording started). A take whose repaint loop and source both stop for three seconds is reported once to the console and to analytics (`camera_frozen`) instead of being published as a static image, and a new fake-media Playwright guard counts the frames a take actually produces so a regression fails the suite ([story](stories/072-fix-frozen-speech-capture/story.md)).
- **Friend closed-response clips play with audio from the first frame** — `SimpleVideoPlayer` auto-play now seeks to 0:00 before `play()`, neutralising the ~1 s edit-list / container-timestamp offset that made the first second silent on initial play (scrubbing to 0:00 already worked) ([story](stories/073-fix-silent-friend-response-playback/story.md)).
- **A short celebration on every friend closed-response step** — when the learner finishes a `friendClosedResponse` step and the app advances to the next one, a short confetti burst plays over the transition (`canvas-confetti@1.6.0`, already a dependency). Five visually distinct effects (side cannons, star shower, fountain, double pop, drifting sparkles) are chosen at random with no immediate repeat, each also randomising its colour palette and its origin/spread/velocity jitter, so the run of 12–40 such steps in a friend lesson never looks like the same fireworks twice; the effects are deliberately short (120–180 ticks, 35–80 particles per burst), silent (a sound at the transition would fight the Android capture-release audio rules), and off entirely for `prefers-reduced-motion`. The rules live in the pure `src/modules/lesson/step-celebration-logic.js`, the confetti firing in `src/modules/lesson/step-celebration.web.js`, and the store trigger (`stepCelebrationTrigger`) fires from `loadNextStep` only for a `friendClosedResponse` step — a monotonic trigger the effect dedupes on, because `React.StrictMode` runs mount effects twice. The success step keeps its existing single fireworks burst and its sound unchanged ([story](stories/092-step-transition-celebration/story.md)).
- **Friend Challenge lessons** — a course's lessons form a ping-pong chain (`wouldyourather`, and the same chain authored for health in `sources/health.source.txt`). The ask lesson lets a learner record "Would you rather?" questions, share a link, and the answer lesson lets a friend answer; the two clips are concatenated. The asker's 3 question clips publish to R2 (48h TTL) and friends answer them via a `?sharecode=` URL.
- **Friend-challenge practice link** — after an ask video is exported, the creator's public profile (`/:shareCode`) shows "Practice English with Me" links to the matching answer lessons, each with a 48h countdown that removes it when the R2 clips expire; there is one link per recorded lesson ([story](stories/012-friend-lesson-link/story.md), [044](stories/044-per-lesson-profile-links/story.md)).
- **Public homepage + user home split** — `/` is a public share-code homepage for everyone (headline, subheadline, input, a Go button, and an "I don't have a share code" button); Go looks the code up first and opens the matching public profile `/:shareCode`, showing an inline "friend code not found" message otherwise, while the no-code button now opens the topic chooser (`/welcome`). The user dashboard (`HomeScreen`: menu, course card, notification bell, Continue) moves to `/home`, post-login/sign-up redirects default to `/home`, and the guest language/login modal no longer opens on `/` ([story](stories/045-homepage-share-code/story.md)).
- **Topic chooser, a page per topic, and a topic drop-down on the course lists** — the home page's "Start without friend's code" button lands on a new inside page (`/welcome`) headed "Choose your topic" (localized into en/es/pt/fr/hi/bn), with one big button per topic. Each button opens that topic's page (`/tags/<topic>`), which lists the courses carrying the topic — the course title, its hero image, and the FULL mission (expanded, not collapsed behind a toggle) — every card linking to the course's first lesson exactly like the course listings page. The course lists themselves (`/home` and `/courses`) carry the same topics as a **drop-down**: a bilingual subheading ("Choose a topic to practice talking about.") and a picker that narrows the list to one topic, in place; its options come from the courses on screen, so it can never offer a topic with no course behind it. A topic is a course-level `tags` array in `src/config/<course>.json`, so there is no separate tag registry: the buttons are the union of the tags the courses carry (the reserved `test` fixtures excluded, so a button never leads to an empty page), matched case-insensitively while the label stays the way the course authored it. **The topic names and the course names are localized, English word first** ("Travel Viajes", "Grocery Shopping Ir de compras"): a learner still learning English may not know what the topic means in English, so the button and the course card carry the English word and the learner's own word beside it. The topic's translated name lives in the strings table (`course_tag_<topic>`); the course name is curriculum content, so it is a locale map in the config itself (`courseName: { en, es, pt, fr, hi, bn }`), and the surfaces that need a plain string (the lesson top bar, the recap, the analytics) use the English one. The topic buttons are client-side links, so the guest language survives the move, and all the pages render inside the app shell with the shared header. **The guest language/login modal is suppressed on the topic pages** (they are public routes, like `/courses`): they are where a visitor without a friend's code goes, and the modal sits in the browser's top layer over the topic buttons — it would stop them picking a topic until they dismissed it. The dashboard `/home` is public for the same reason: it shows the same course list with the same topic drop-down, and the modal covered that drop-down too. Those pages adopt the browser language silently when the guest has not chosen one (the guest's own choice always wins), so a visitor who opens a topic page — or the course list — directly still reads it in their language.
- **A real 404 for unrecognized URLs** — an unknown path now renders a localized 404 page (warning glyph, "Page not found" copy, and links to `/courses` and `/`) instead of being silently bounced to `/`; a single-segment path is still routed to `/:shareCode` but only renders the profile when the segment has the generator's share-code shape (case-insensitive — an uppercase or mixed-case link is lowercased to the stored code — `ALPHABET` of 35 chars `abcdefghijkmnopqrstuvwxyz0123456789`, four or five characters, the latter for all new codes), so `/nonsense` shows the 404 rather than "User not found" while real links (`abcd`, `k9gyi`, `/Abcd`) are unchanged. A shape-valid but unknown code keeps the existing "User not found" page, because the visitor did aim at a profile ([story](stories/071-real-404-page/story.md)).
- **Bootstrap homepage (homepage only)** — the public homepage `/` is the one route built with real **Bootstrap 5.3.8** (navbar/container grid, cards, list-group, forms, buttons, `animate.css` entrance animations, bootstrap-icons). The vendored stylesheet is rewritten under a single root class (`.uff-home-bs`) by `scripts/lib/bootstrap-homepage-css.js` — every selector scoped, `:root`/`html`/`body` reboots collapsed onto the scope element, `@keyframes` renamed — and regenerated into the committed `src/generated/homepage-bootstrap.css` by a Vite plugin, so no other route is ever restyled. The palette is the main app's own (Bootstrap `--bs-*` variables set to `#0b1a2a`/`#1a3a5a`/`#2a4a6a`/`#adb5bd`/the `#3a8fd5→#00c0d8` brand gradient, `.btn-uff-primary` CTA). On desktop the page renders as a normal Bootstrap page — `#root:has(> .uff-home-bs)` lifts the 9:16 phone frame the rest of the app uses, while `/home`, `/courses`, `/:shareCode` and the lesson routes keep it ([story](stories/059-homepage-landing-sections/story.md)).
- **Homepage marketing sections in the app's own style** — the public homepage (`/`) scrolls below the share-code box into a "How it works" card (five bullets drawn from the default `share_message`: free/no card, any level, no need to be online at the same time, instant speaking feedback, 48-hour window), a "See what a conversation looks like" carousel of curated concatenated conversation videos (permanent `assets/videos/` slugs; the poster shows first and the video loads only on play), and an "About the teacher" card for Joe Walsh (TESOL master's, 20 years' teaching) showing his `teacherprofile.webp` photo; the top bar gains a language selector (`HOME_LANGUAGES`: en/es/pt/fr/hi/bn) that sets the page language, and an anonymous visitor's supported browser language is adopted silently on `/` (English, an unsupported language, a logged-in user, or an already-chosen language is unchanged). Every control and section uses the app's tokens (`#0b1a2a` page, `#1a3a5a` cards, the `#3a8fd5→#00c0d8` gradient primary Go button, an outlined secondary no-code button, Inter font), and the new copy is localized into en/es/pt/fr/hi/bn ([story](stories/059-homepage-landing-sections/story.md)).
- **Friend-response notifications** — when a friend opens an asker's share link and exports the answer video, the asker gets an in-app notification in a bell menu on the home screen, with a timestamp and a link to the friend's public profile (there is no "respond within 48 hours" line: the chain's closing lesson produces a notification with nothing left to answer, and a line that promises a reply the product cannot deliver is worse than no line) (`/<shareCode>`); notifications are stored in Supabase, deduped per friend, and mark-as-read ([story](stories/019-notify-share-link-video-created/story.md)).
- **Responders become challengers** — legacy behavior in the friend course (`src/config/wouldyourather.json`) only: the ask questions are appended to the end of answer lesson `b` (tagged `publishLessonId: "a"`), so the same `b` export publishes the responder's own question clips under lesson `a`. The chain code no longer consults `askPublished`; friend lessons after the first chain lesson (`a`) now create their profile link directly ([story](stories/015-b-lesson-ask-clips/story.md)).
- **Friend-challenge chain** — friend lessons form an ordered ping-pong `a, b, c, …`: the chain is the `recapOverlay: "shareCta"` lessons in `configData.lessons` order, finishing lesson X records a profile share link pointing at X+1, a friend-response notification fires for every chain lesson after the first, and the public profile lists one labelled link per in-flight lesson. **Lesson titles are generic and positional** (`Preferences A`…`G`, localized in all six locales), because the profile labels a link by its POSITION in the chain -- "Lesson 3 of 7", derived from the course config at render time -- and a title that named its topics went stale the moment the lesson was rewritten. **The chain ends with a closing lesson that says goodbye**: every lesson answers the friend's clip AND records the next question set, so the last pair has a third lesson -- the closing one -- which answers the final set and has no next set to record; its second half is one rung branch plus four text-only sign-off takes (code-owned by the generator, like the success step; the sheet authors questions, not the chain's sign-off). Friend mode never auto-advances — `nextLessonId` stays single-user-only ([story](stories/043-extend-friend-lesson-chain/story.md)).
- **Friend-aware Continue on `/home`** — the dashboard Continue keeps its exact solo resume link for single-user courses, but a `shareCta` resume resolves friend-first: the oldest still-live (< 48h) friend response lesson as a direct `/course/<course>/<next>?shareCode=<friend>` deep-link (href always from the friend's `friend_links` entry, never the notification row), else the `a` lesson of a randomly chosen unstarted friend course (stable across re-renders), else a started non-friend resume, else the button hides ([story](stories/061-continue-button-friend-link/story.md)). On `shareCta` success rows (Share-only today) the existing Continue appears in the same row 3s after the Share tap, whatever the delivery outcome; non-friend success rows are unchanged.
- **Per-lesson profile links** — the public profile lists one link per lesson whose prompts the owner recorded (not one per course), grouped under a course-name heading. Each link is labeled with the recorded lesson's **position in its chain** ("Lesson 3 of 6", localized to the visitor, computed from the course config at read time -- the stored English title is only the fallback while the config loads, because a title frozen at export time kept showing the old wording after a rename) and opens the follow-on lesson with the owner's `?shareCode=`, so a visitor can pick the prompt set they remember. Entries are keyed `courseId:recordedLessonId` in the `friend_links` jsonb map and expire on the same 48h window; the "already played" state relies on the browser cache, with no new storage ([story](stories/044-per-lesson-profile-links/story.md)).
- **Visitor-aware public profile** — the public profile (`/:shareCode`) prioritizes lessons involving a logged-in visitor (entries featuring them come first) and marks a lesson the visitor already did with the owner: "Completed it together ✅" badge plus a struck-through lesson name, so it is clear they need not repeat it; logged-out visitors see every recorded link plus a login/guest banner. The badge is PAIR-scoped: it appears only when the visitor completed the lesson the LINK OPENS (`lessonId`) **with this owner** — not on the owner's recorded lesson (only the owner's own history holds it, which made the badge appear only on the visitor's own profile) and not merely because the visitor's own completion history contains that lesson (which badges a lesson done with a DIFFERENT friend, and a lesson is expected to be repeated with several friends). The pairing is recorded server-side in `friend_completions` by the same completion RPC that credits the friendship (`record_friend_completion`), and read with `actor_id = viewer` AND `owner_id = profile` under an actor-only RLS policy ([story](stories/064-profile-visitor-lessons/story.md)). **When every OPEN link is already badged for the visitor, the list is replaced by a variant of the empty state** — there is nothing left for them to answer, so the card says "There are no open lessons right now. You've already completed them together — start a brand new lesson in a new course and send them the link." (`profile_friend_lessons_all_completed`, all six locales) with the same "Start here" link into `/courses`; the variant is pair-scoped like the badge, so it never appears because a lesson was done with a different friend. The friend-practice heading and subheading are localized in the strings table (`profile_friend_links_heading` / `_subheading`, with the owner's name as a `{name}` placeholder) and read in the VISITOR's language, not hardcoded English in the component. **The banner's "no account yet" line is plain text, not a link** — it used to be an anchor that jumped to the block of lesson links directly beneath it; it now reads "No account yet? Start practicing free. Click one of the links below to start." and points the visitor at those links ([story](stories/080-profile-guest-note-plain-text/story.md)). **Every page but the landing page and the lesson player now shows the app's shared header** — the brand bar (menu button, UFF logo, notification bell) with the slide-out drawer — including the public profile, the 404 page and the auth pages, so a friend link no longer lands on a page that looks like a different app ([story](stories/081-app-header-every-page/story.md)). **The profile shows every link the learner recorded, and a finished lesson is counted** — the profile used to hide every link whose lesson was not the course's first (so a learner who recorded the follow-up lesson had no link at all, and the page said "all your lessons have expired" while a fresh link sat in the database), and the lesson counters were written by a client-side read-modify-write that could be lost silently; the counters now come from a server-side RPC keyed on the session, so a finished lesson always shows on the learner's own profile ([story](stories/082-profile-counts-and-links/story.md)). **The follow-up link is recorded server-side** — the link a learner sends to their friends after a friend lesson is written by the `record_friend_link` RPC (SECURITY DEFINER, atomic merge, owner = `auth.uid()`) instead of a client-side read-modify-write of the whole `friend_links` map, which could lose the link SILENTLY (a zero-row UPDATE is not an error in PostgREST) and leave the profile saying "all your lessons have expired" right after the learner recorded one ([story](stories/078-record-friend-link-server-side/story.md)).
- **The public profile reads in the visitor's language** — the public profile is localized to the **visitor's** session language (the app's guest-first `guestNativeLanguage || userData.native_language || 'en'`, the same resolution the homepage and the 404 page use), never to the language of the profile being read: a Spanish learner opening a Hindi speaker's profile reads the heading, the stats, the friend-practice area and the "user not found" state in Spanish, and the "member since" date is formatted in the visitor's locale. The owner's own `native_language` is no longer read by the page ([story](stories/077-public-profile-visitor-language/story.md)).
- **Every learner in the chain sees the friend's clip** — a chain clip is addressed by its **recorder** alone (`<recorder>-<recorder>-…`): the learner who opens the recorder's link is the one who computes the key it looks for, and that learner knows the recorder's code and nothing else. The app resolves a friend reference to `<friend>-<friend>` and publishes every clip under the recorder's own name, so **each hop** of the chain plays — the asker's own clip and the previous learner's copy of the same question set — for a signed-in visitor, an anonymous visitor and a stranger alike ([story](stories/083-friend-clip-addressing/story.md)). The ask lesson's own special case (the asker started the chain, so there is no inviter) is now the general rule ([story](stories/079-friend-video-key-inviter/story.md)).
- **Inviting friend-practice area + public course listings** — the public profile's friend-challenge area leads with a "Practice English with Me Free" header and a "click on a lesson link to start." subheading, shows each course's name as a large heading over a bulleted list of underlined lesson links (each keeping its 48h countdown), and — when no links are active — replaces the course area with an "all lessons expired after 48 hours" message and a large "Practice English Free" link. - **Auto-advancing friend answers** — answering a friend's question correctly advances straight to the next step with no feedback pause; incorrect answers retry via the hangman hint until correct ([story](stories/005-skip-friend-feedback/story.md)).
- **Friend prompts survive click-through presentation** — when a friend-challenge lesson shows the friend's clip as a non-interactive click-through step before the explanatory response step, the recap still concatenates the friend's question clips onto the recorded answer ([story](stories/016-recap-friend-prompt-clips/story.md)).
- **Low-friction friend lessons** — a lesson opened via a friend link (`?shareCode=`) or with a single-letter chain lesson id (`a`–`z`) never auto-opens the guest login step: a non-English browser language is adopted silently, and only an English browser sees the language-confirmation step (which then closes, no Log In / Sign Up prompt). - **Simple-video response decision overlay** — a response step (`closedResponse`/`openResponse`/`friendClosedResponse`) whose only clip is a `simpleVideoUrl` hides the mic while the clip plays (freeing the bottom band for its subtitles) and then, once the clip ends or fails to load, raises the Replay / Answer / Tutorial water overlay; closed responses use the "repeat exactly" copy and label, open responses the "did you understand?" copy and "respond now" label ([story](stories/028-friend-response-video-overlay/story.md)). - **Branching question steps** — a `branching` step plays its `simpleVideoUrl` clip and then raises a water overlay with a vertically stacked column of text buttons in the bottom middle (Replay left / Watch Tutorial right corners kept), each jumping straight to a chosen step by a relative `chooseStep[].nextStep` offset; the branch step itself records and scores nothing, and a new optional per-step `nextStep` offset (honored by `loadNextStep`, default +1) lets the alternative steps converge on one shared continuation instead of falling into each other. Supabase accounts get a shareCode, profile, and R2 publish rights. A language the guest chooses (or that is silently adopted) is authoritative for the session and survives the asynchronous profile bootstrap, so the whole app — including the recap — stays in that language ([story](stories/023-guest-language-survives-bootstrap-profile-write/story.md)).

Continue without translations." ([story](stories/037-guest-language-modal-copy/story.md)). The step and the login-choice step carry the same languages (en/es/pt/fr/hi/bn), and the modal is suppressed on profile pages (the private `/profile` and the public `/:shareCode`).
- **Pre-video signup gate** — the signup modal shown when a guest presses create-video hides its "Not now" button via CSS (the element stays in the DOM), so the modal reads as a signup gate; the first/last name fields in both signup forms align with the full-width email/password fields because the missing `.col-6`/`.col-md-6`/`.mt-md-0` grid classes are added to the hand-written stylesheet ([story](stories/034-hide-signup-skip-button/story.md)).
- **Legal links on every sign-up surface** — the pre-video signup modal, the guest login modal (both steps), and the standalone `/signup` form all show localized Privacy Policy / Terms of Service links in small text at the bottom of the form/modal; the links open the public legal pages in a new tab so the in-progress signup is not covered by the globally-mounted modal ([story](stories/050-signup-modal-legal-links/story.md)).
- **Zero-cost signup email screen** — signup screens the address before any account is created, rejecting what is *certain* to bounce: a malformed address, a throwaway provider, a high-confidence misspelling (the message suggests the real domain), and a domain with no mail route (nonexistent, RFC 7505 Null MX, or no MX and no address record — with RFC 5321 implicit MX honored so a deliverable MX-less domain is not wrongly rejected). It costs nothing: the one network call is Cloudflare's public DNS-over-HTTPS resolver (keyless, unmetered), and syntax/disposable/typo rules are pure and offline, so there is no API key, no account, no dependency and no environment variable. It fails **open** on every uncertainty (SERVFAIL, timeout, unreadable body, non-ASCII domain) so a resolver fault cannot cost a signup, and only deterministic verdicts block. Mailbox-level verification is impossible here by platform (Workers cannot open outbound TCP on port 25) and would not prove existence anyway (catch-all domains, providers hiding it) — the authoritative proof remains the existing `/confirm-email` double opt-in ([story](stories/069-zero-cost-email-validation/story.md), [docs](docs/email-validation.md)).
- **Lazy signup — the account is created at the end of the first lesson** — the standalone `/signup` form is closed: `/signup` redirects to `/courses`, and every entry point into it (the guest modal's Sign Up button, the login page's sign-up link, the guest profile's account button) now goes to the course list instead. Nothing is deleted — `SignupRoute` and `SignupForm.web.jsx` stay resident and tested, so reopening is a one-component change; this is an experiment with a plausible near-term reversal. The account itself is created by the form the success screen already rendered (`SuccessButtons` → `SaveClipsModal` → `SaveClipsSignupForm`). The barrier is not merely "later in the flow": a friend lesson is **video-only** (`IntroChoices` withholds text and audio-only), so that form is unreachable without a camera and microphone stream, where the closed form was three POST-able fields. `/courses` now carries a signed-out notice ("You're not signed in" → sign-in link → "complete a mini lesson and create your free account at the end"), and the first numbered step names the account. Honest limits: `--use-fake-device-for-media-stream` satisfies `getUserMedia`, so the gate is expensive for a spammer, not proof of a human; the conversion cost is a guess, not a measurement (the account population is far too small to price it), and the modal form still runs with `captcha: false` — adding Turnstile is the follow-up ([story](stories/070-lazy-signup-funnel/story.md)).
- **Bounded memory across repeated voice answers** — the unused per-step recording-chunk copy is dropped and the Whisper engine-readiness poll is cleared on step change, so repeated mic taps in a long lesson can no longer leave stale polling intervals running ([story](stories/038-fix-whisper-repeated-use-slowdown/story.md)).
- **Cloud lesson-video pipeline (authoring)** — the operator's phone recorder uploads takes to Cloudflare R2 (`raw/`), an operator-key-protected Pages Function starts a Modal render (reusing the existing T4 BiRefNet function) for a filename set, and the Modal app runs the silence/background/render stages, publishing `assets/videos/<slug>.mp4` plus a poster and writing a status marker the recorder polls; this is the teacher-media authoring path, not learner recaps, which stay client-side ([story](stories/040-modal-lesson-video-pipeline/story.md)).
- **Raw takes are private** — unpublished phone takes, pipeline-asset inputs and the render status marker live in a separate, non-public R2 bucket (`PIPELINE_R2` binding, default `uff-private`) that the public CDN `r2.ultrafastfluency.com` cannot serve; only published `assets/videos/...` and learner/UGC `videos/...` stay in the public `uff` bucket. Raw keys self-expire via an R2 lifecycle rule while `videos/` keeps its 48h TTL ([story](stories/041-private-raw-takes/story.md)).
- **Course configs generated from the committed master CSV** — an operator-invoked Node CLI (`scripts/generate-config-from-sheet.mjs`, no new deps) reads every committed `public/*.master.csv` and writes a **new** `src/config/<courseId>.json` per course from explicit structural CSV columns; it **overwrites** an existing `src/config/<courseId>.json` (the CSV is authoritative — no `--force` gate); its output is English-only unless the CSV carries the per-language columns (the [049](stories/049-translate-sheet/story.md) translation round-trip fills `*_es/_pt/_bn/_fr/_hi`, which the generator then emits as `{en,es,pt,bn,fr,hi}`), so the committed CSV is the source of truth for both videos and configs ([042](stories/042-generate-config-from-sheet/story.md), [047](stories/047-overwrite-configs/story.md)).
- **Auto-generated configs, one per course, committed by CI** — a GitHub Action (`.github/workflows/configs.yml`, run via the "Generate Course Configs" workflow button, no local commands) generates one `src/config/<courseId>.json` per course the CSV defines (partitioned by `course_id`) and commits any changes; it is best-effort — a course with any missing required column is skipped whole and reported (which columns, where) without blocking the other courses or the video render, and the same workflow already accepts a `repository_dispatch (render-complete)` event for a later hands-off trigger ([story](stories/046-auto-generate-configs/story.md)).
- **Course configs auto-committed by GitHub Actions** — a `configs.yml` action (manual "Run workflow" now; prepared for a later Modal→GitHub `repository_dispatch`) regenerates configs from the committed CSV and commits them with the captions-bot pattern (PAT checkout, `[skip configs]` loop guard, commit only on a diff). A CSV may define **several courses**: each is generated independently into its own `src/config/<courseId>.json`, and a course whose required columns are incomplete is **skipped whole** (never a partial config) with a precise `SKIP course "…": missing required column(s): …` report; skips are non-fatal so the already-completed video render is never blocked ([story](stories/046-auto-generate-configs/story.md)).
- **The CSV is authoritative — generated configs overwrite** — a CSV-defined course's `src/config/<courseId>.json` is regenerated and **overwritten unconditionally** on every run (the `--force` gate and flag are gone); the CI action therefore propagates later CSV edits to an already-generated course, and the write goes through a same-directory temp file + rename so a killed run cannot leave a half-written config. Because the CSV now carries per-language columns (the [049](stories/049-translate-sheet/story.md) round-trip fills `*_es/_pt/_bn/_fr/_hi`), an overwrite keeps the CSV's es/pt/bn/fr/hi translations; a hand edit straight to a repo JSON still loses it on the next run — an accepted consequence of "the CSV is the source of truth", not a guarded refusal ([story](stories/047-overwrite-configs/story.md)).
- **The generated config is the ABABAB shape, and no course is hard-coded** — the generator builds each lesson from the course's PAIRS: every lesson after the first is respond-then-record (the friend's clip opens the lesson, the learner answers it, then the learner records the next set's question videos through a branching step that offers the four phrasings). The chain is DERIVED from the pair order, so the author writes every phrase exactly once in `<course>.source.txt` and the bridge flattens it into the CSV's structural columns (`pair_index`, `set_role`, `topic_index`, `group_index`, `variant_index`). A step is one TAKE (the `-NN` suffixed video the app plays), never the take-group, and its subtitle is its own PHRASE with no timings — the sheet's `srt` describes the group, and the player shows a plain phrase for the video's whole length. A new course is one `<course>.source.txt` away: the bridge derives `public/<course>.master.csv` from the source name, the generator globs every master CSV, the translator and the srt writer loop over all of them, and the recorder takes the course from `?course=` ([story](stories/076-generate-ababab-config/story.md)).
- **Master-CSV translations round-trip (en→es/pt/bn/fr/hi) committed by a GitHub Action** — the committed `public/wouldyourather.master.csv` holds the English source columns (`lesson_title`, `mission`, `cue`, `cue_alt`, `choose_step_text`, `subtitle_text`, `srt`) next to their per-language columns; a manual `workflow_dispatch` Action (`.github/workflows/translate-master.yml`) translates any **blank** target cells with the Gemini client (`translateText` for plain cells, `translateSrt` for `srt` cue text — cue numbers and timestamps preserved, validated by `validateTranslatedSrt`; `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` with free-tier retry, taught English target phrase preserved), and commits them back into the file (PAT checkout, `[skip translate]` loop guard, commit only on a diff) as per-language columns (`lesson_title_es`, `mission_pt`, `cue_es`, `cue_alt_es`, `choose_step_text_es`, `subtitle_text_bn`, `srt_es`, `phrase_es`, … — one column per language per field: the 35 authoring columns, plus 5 `phrase_<lang>` for the overlay master, 40 in all). The generator then reads those columns into `{en,es,pt,bn,fr,hi}` objects on `title`/`mission`/`cue`/`chooseStep[].text`/`subtitles` (so localized video captions come from `srt_<lang>`), and the CSV now holds the translations the app localizes from. Translation only fills blanks (idempotent), so a human edit to a translation cell is honored by the next config generation; the English source is never overwritten. The one exception is `srt`: the render pipeline rewrites the English `srt` on every re-render, so a stored `srt_<lang>` whose cue count or timings no longer match the current English SRT is treated as stale and re-translated (logged as `STALE …`) — a hand-edit that keeps the cue count and timings is still honored ([story](stories/049-translate-sheet/story.md)).
- **One committed master CSV drives both the video pipeline and the app config** — the overlay master (`title_text, Order, phrase, subtitle_text, join, video_file, …`, read by the recorder and `video_pipeline.py`) gains explicit config columns (`course_id, course_name, lesson_id, lesson_title, response_type, unit, mission, recap_sources, recap_overlay, intro_video, success_video, success_srt, srt`) and becomes the single source of truth. The config generator detects the master shape (`phrase` header), groups steps by `join` (the joined video is the step and the published slug), builds the app **cue as an ordered array** from the per-row `phrase` values (basic→advanced; the app already picks element [0] and matches against all), takes app subtitles from the pipeline-generated `srt` only (never the burnt-in overlay `subtitle_text`), and synthesizes the `lessonIntro`/`success` steps from the explicit columns. All three consumers (generator `MASTER_CSV_GLOB`, translator, recorder relative path) read every committed `public/*.master.csv`, and the pipeline's computed SRT is persisted to R2 and merged back into the CSV's `srt` column by a GitHub Action for record-keeping ([story](stories/050-master-sheet-config/story.md)).
- **CSV-authored branching choices** — the master CSV gains step-level `next_step`, `choose_step_next`, and `choose_step_text` columns (plus `choose_step_text_es/_pt/_bn/_fr/_hi` filled by the translate round-trip); the generator emits the app's `nextStep`/`chooseStep` fields and validates them loudly — mismatched line counts, a non-integer or `< 1` offset, or `choose_step_*` on a non-`branching` step leave that course ungenerated (other courses unaffected) — so a fork and its converging alternative steps are authored in the CSV rather than by hand ([story](stories/055-sheet-branching-authoring/story.md)).
- **Cloud render reuses the deployed BiRefNet GPU app** — because Modal now requires a payment method to deploy a *new* persistent GPU function, the lesson pipeline no longer registers a T4 function on its own app (`uff-lesson-video`); it calls the already-deployed `video-background-removal` BiRefNet function by name, so `modal deploy docs/video-pipeline/modal_app.py` ships only CPU functions (background removal is unchanged). The GPU function's definition lives in `docs/video-pipeline/background_removal_app.py`, which is not part of the deploy graph ([story](stories/053-reuse-deployed-bg-removal/story.md)).
- **One-click pipeline: a render runs SRT write-back → translation → config generation automatically** — after the Modal render finishes it dispatches a `repository_dispatch (render-complete)`; a new `pipeline.yml` chains the three existing Actions as reusable-workflow jobs (`needs:`), and every job reads the committed CSV from a fresh checkout, so the chain has no publish lag. The operator's only action is to record takes and press render; the course ends up configured and localized with no further clicks ([story](stories/051-one-click-pipeline/story.md)).
- **The render reads the committed master CSV directly** — the Modal orchestrator fetches the CSV over HTTP (`master_csv_url()` in `pipeline_lib.py`, parity-guarded against the generator/recorder; `PIPELINE_SHEET_URL` override) as its `video_data.csv` input, instead of downloading a hand-uploaded `pipeline-assets/video_data.csv`. `pipeline-assets/video_data.csv` becomes a post-render output (the CSV plus the computed `srt` column that the sync-srt Action reads), so the committed file is the single source and nothing is uploaded to R2 by hand ([story](stories/052-sheet-backed-render-input/story.md)).
- **Lesson-level auto-render in the recorder** — after each take upload the recorder checks that take's lesson and, when every filename-bearing row of the lesson is uploaded, auto-renders just that lesson as its own Modal job (re-takes re-trigger only their lesson; a manual full-course START RENDER remains as the escape hatch), so a preemption loses at most one lesson's container work instead of the whole 108-take session ([story](stories/066-recorder-auto-render-lessons/story.md)).
- **Per-step-type tutorial modal with localized subtitles** — the tutorial button on the view-and-continue, response, and branching decision rows opens a full-screen scrollable modal with a corner X styled like the app close X, a portrait `720×1280` tutorial video unique to the step type (`assets/videos/tutorial-<stepType>.mp4` for the 5 tutorial-bearing types), app-rendered subtitles in the guest-first session language (es/pt/fr translations of the script, English fallback), and a localized Continue button under the video; X, Continue, backdrop click, and Escape all dismiss and the lesson underneath pauses ([story](stories/061-tutorial-modal/story.md)).

## Non-Goals

- Native mobile apps — the codebase targets the web; React Native variants exist but are not shipped.
- No text-mode publishing path — typed answers are a practice fallback, never shareable UGC.
- Real-time human tutoring or live conversation.
- Authoring tools for teachers; lesson content is authored in a `<course>.source.txt` per course (`sources/wouldyourather.source.txt`, `sources/health.source.txt`), regenerated into that course's master CSV by CI.
- No full localization beyond en/es/pt/fr (plus targeted hi/bn keys); profile supports many more locales than the UI is translated into.
- No server-side video rendering — recaps are generated entirely client-side.

## Known Limitations

- Text-mode users cannot publish clips (null blobs are filtered from the R2 export), so a friend opening their share URL gets a 404 lesson; the share CTA still renders over their avatar-card recap.
- `hi`/`bn` are partial localizations — most UI strings fall back to English for those users.
- Speech recognition runs Whisper in-browser and requires COOP/COEP and cross-origin headers; videos and Whisper fail on staging without the Cloudflare Transform Rules (`docs/cloudflare-video-cors.md`).
- Mic/camera cannot be exercised headlessly; many flows require the documented test bypasses (`AGENTS.md`).
- Lesson media is hosted on Cloudflare R2 (`assets/videos/<slug>.mp4`); video files are gitignored (too large for the repository) and uploaded out of band, so lesson video playback depends on R2 availability and on the real recordings having been uploaded.
- Lesson-intro posters follow the same `<video>.jpg` sibling rule and are R2-only (`assets/videos/<slug>.jpg` for teacher intros, `videos/<…>.jpg` for UGC); teacher posters are written by the render (`modal_app._publish` for step/join slugs, `_publish_intro_posters` for `intro_video` slugs) and UGC posters at publish, so nothing is committed, served locally, or regenerated by the deploy, and a poster is only as available as R2 ([story](stories/011-auto-intro-poster/story.md)).
- The lesson-intro overlay no longer shows a caller name/title — the hardcoded "Joe Walsh / English Coach, UFF" was removed because it is wrong for friend/UGC intros. Surfacing the friend's own name (from their profile, via the share code) is deferred ([story](stories/013-remove-intro-caller-name/story.md)).
- Playwright's bundled Chromium lacks H.264/AAC codecs; video specs must run real Chrome (`AGENTS.md` — Infrastructure Gotchas).
- The R2 UGC 48h TTL is configured in the Cloudflare dashboard, not in `wrangler.toml` (`README.md:98`).
- Classroom/staging testing depends on R2 availability; video playback cannot be fully verified offline.
- "Friend lesson" detection is route-based (`?shareCode=` in the URL, or a single-letter lesson id `a`–`z`) and does not include the course id, because course ids are open-ended. As a result any future course with a single-letter lesson id would be treated as a friend lesson: it would skip the automatic guest login/language prompt and be restricted to video-only mode ([story](stories/016-friend-lesson-modals/story.md), [020](stories/020-friend-video-only/story.md), [043](stories/043-extend-friend-lesson-chain/story.md)). The known single-user course is renamed off single letters (`model`), so none is affected today.
- The friend-challenge chain is derived generically from `recapOverlay: "shareCta"` lessons in config order. `model.json` is a single-user course and contains no `shareCta` lessons, so it never chains ([story](stories/043-extend-friend-lesson-chain/story.md)).
- The health course (`src/config/health.json`) is authored, localized and listed, but its media does not exist yet: the takes are recorded and rendered by the operator, and until the render publishes them `health-a-01-01.mp4/.jpg` are absent from R2 (so the lesson player has no video and `scripts/verify-thumbnails.mjs` reports the missing intro poster). No video is committed and no placeholder is generated ([story](stories/089-health-course/story.md)).
- A guest who silently adopts a browser language on a friend lesson and then logs in within the same SPA session can keep that adopted language in memory until the next full page load; a page load while already logged in uses the profile language and skips all friend-lesson logic ([story](stories/016-friend-lesson-modals/story.md)).
- The automatic poster-to-profile-picture assignment only fills a null/placeholder slot; a stored `profile_picture_url` that is non-null but broken (e.g. a legacy `appwrite.io` URL) is treated as "has a picture" and is not replaced — a separate cleanup would be needed ([story](stories/019-use-poster-as-profile-picture/story.md)).
- The concatenated recap upload is best-effort and capped at the Cloudflare Function's 50 MB per-object limit; a longer lesson whose stitched mp4 exceeds 50 MB is silently skipped (logged + tracked), so its complete video is not published even though its per-segment clips are ([story](stories/021-upload-concatenated-videos-to-r2/story.md)).
- Recorded-clip subtitles burn the cue only for closed-response steps; an open-response answer has no canonical cue to match and still burns the transcript ([story](stories/028-burn-cue-not-transcription/story.md)).
- Per-segment clip publishing (trim, transcode and upload) completes while the page stays alive, including backgrounded or unfocused; if the OS force-closes, freezes, or discards the tab before it finishes, the remaining clips are not uploaded, and there is no cross-browser background-sync or resume path ([story](stories/035-single-pass-clip-publish/story.md)).
- Per-segment clips are cut from the recap recording using wall-clock step time ranges calibrated against the recording duration; a boundary miscalibration could trim a word, so the cut points are validated on real devices ([story](stories/035-single-pass-clip-publish/story.md)).
- A friend clip has no burned-in caption when its asker recorded before the caption rule or never matched a cue, so its recap segment then shows no subtitle; the current user's cue is deliberately never used as a fallback on the friend's clip ([story](stories/036-fix-friend-lesson-subtitles/story.md)).
- The burned recap/UGC subtitle's translation is computed at answer time with the same `guest-first session language` the recap uses (`guestNativeLanguage || userData.native_language || 'en'`), so a guest/adopted session language no longer leaves a clip with an English-only burn while the recap is localized; a recording made before this fix keeps its English-only burn until it is re-recorded ([story](stories/058-fix-ugc-clip-translation-subtitles/story.md)).
